# Where theological literature lives

A starting map, not a census. "Free" means the venue posts full text openly, which matters when fetch works: one table-of-contents page beats ten searches. Verify any detail before citing it; publishers move.

## Journals by tradition

**Reformed and confessional evangelical**: Westminster Theological Journal; Journal of the Evangelical Theological Society (JETS; free PDFs at etsjets.org after a delay, indexed at galaxie.com); Themelios (free, at thegospelcoalition.org); Calvin Theological Journal; Trinity Journal; Southern Baptist Journal of Theology; Mid-America Journal of Theology; Puritan Reformed Journal; Presbyterion (Covenant Seminary); Reformed Faith & Practice (RTS, free at journal.rts.edu); Unio cum Christo (free); Ordained Servant (OPC, free at opc.org); Reformed Theological Review (Australia); Scottish Bulletin of Evangelical Theology; Churchman; Evangelical Quarterly; Foundations (Affinity, free); The Confessional Presbyterian; Journal of Reformed Theology (Brill); Bibliotheca Sacra (DTS); Criswell Theological Review; Journal for Baptist Theology & Ministry (free); Eusebeia; Haddington House Journal; Reformed Theological Journal (Belfast); Theologia Reformata (Dutch).

**Mainline and academic**: International Journal of Systematic Theology (Wiley); Scottish Journal of Theology (Cambridge); Modern Theology; Pro Ecclesia (Sage); Journal of Theological Studies; Theology Today; Interpretation; Anglican Theological Review; Reviews in Religion & Theology (Wiley; reviews only); Religious Studies Review; Theological Studies; New Blackfriars; Nova et Vetera; The Thomist; Heythrop Journal.

**Pentecostal and charismatic**: Pneuma (Brill; its cumulative book-review index is a PDF at sps-usa.org); Journal of Pentecostal Theology (Brill); Australasian Pentecostal Studies (free); Journal of the European Pentecostal Theological Association; Spiritus. When a Reformed author engages Pentecostal theology, a Pentecostal review is the most informative reception evidence and is often reposted at pentecostaltheology.com.

**Lutheran**: Concordia Theological Quarterly (free at ctsfw.net); Concordia Journal (free); Logia; Lutheran Quarterly.

**Biblical studies**: Journal of Biblical Literature; New Testament Studies; Catholic Biblical Quarterly; JSNT; JSOT; Bulletin for Biblical Research; Tyndale Bulletin (free); Novum Testamentum; Review of Biblical Literature (free, reviews).

**Philosophy of religion and philosophical theology**: Faith and Philosophy; Religious Studies; International Journal for Philosophy of Religion; Philosophia Christi; TheoLogica (free); Journal of Analytic Theology (free).

**Magazines and semi-scholarly outlets**: Modern Reformation (free archive at modernreformation.org); Credo Magazine (free); Tabletalk (Ligonier); First Things; Touchstone; Comment; Mere Orthodoxy; Christianity Today.

## Review outlets on the web

The Gospel Coalition (reviews and Themelios); Reformation21; Books at a Glance; 9Marks; Ligonier; Credo; Englewood Review of Books; Reading Religion (AAR, free); Heidelblog; Scriptorium Daily (Fred Sanders); Reformed Forum; individual scholars' blogs. Goodreads and Amazon are pointers only, never sources.

## Finders

- Google Scholar: the "cited by" list is the fastest reception census when fetch works.
- Crossref (api.crossref.org/works?query=), OpenAlex (api.openalex.org/works?search=, with cited_by filters), Semantic Scholar (api.semanticscholar.org): bibliographic APIs; check them in the preflight.
- ATLA Religion Database (via the user's library) is the authority for which journals reviewed a book; web search only shows what is indexed.
- Dissertations: ProQuest; institutional repositories (repository.sbts.edu, WTS, DTS, TEDS, Aberdeen, Edinburgh, St Andrews, EThOS); TREN.
- Publisher pages carry endorsements, tables of contents, and sometimes sample chapters; Zondervan Academic, Baker Academic, Crossway, IVP Academic, Eerdmans, T&T Clark, Brill, Oxford, Cambridge.
- Conference programs: ETS annual meeting program PDFs list plenaries and dedicated sessions on a book; WordMp3 sells the audio and its listings name the sessions.
- Lecture series that become books: Moore College Annual Lectures (Sydney; Preservica repository), Kantzer Lectures (TEDS), Warfield Lectures (Princeton), Stone Lectures, Gifford Lectures, Hayward Lectures (Acadia), Payton Lectures (Fuller), Gay Lectures (SBTS), Norton Lectures, Laidlaw Lectures, Didsbury Lectures. The lecture titles are the compressed form of the book.
- Podcasts and interviews where an author states aims: White Horse Inn, Office Hours (WSC), Reformed Forum, Credo, Mortification of Spin, Theology on the Go, RTS podcasts, Ligonier, seminary YouTube channels.
